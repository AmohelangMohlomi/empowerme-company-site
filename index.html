Who we are

EmpowerMe Youth initiative is a registered non-profit company that aims to empower high school students and young adults to succeed in their academic and professional pursuits. Our organization is dedicated to bridge the gap between education and the real world by providing a comprehensive support system that fosters a mindset shift from employment to entrepreneurship

Our mission

To bridge the gap between education and the real world by fostering entrepreneurial thinking, self-reliance, and innovation among young people.

Our approach

Real experiences, free online resources and hands-on workshops, delivered through genuine connection.

Our promise

Every student is prepared for tertiary education, knowing how to apply and what to expect.

What we do

These are the strategies the EmpowerMe Youth Initiative will employ to achieve our goals:

Mentorship Programs:

Connect students with experienced mentors to provide guidance and inspiration.

Workshops and Seminars:

Host engaging sessions on entrepreneurship, career exploration, and personal development.

Online Resources:

Launch a user-friendly platform offering tools, templates, and guidance on academic and professional pathways.

Community Engagement:

Partner with local businesses, schools, and community organizations to create hands-on opportunities for growth and skill-building.
